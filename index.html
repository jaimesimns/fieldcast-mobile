About

Fieldcast is a location-based listening experience exploring how landscapes carry stories across geological, ecological, and human timescales.

As visitors move around Maastricht, GPS automatically triggers audio soundscapes connected to their surroundings.

Rather than following a fixed route, visitors discover stories by entering listening zones.
