About

A listening
experience.

AAR is a location-based listening experience exploring how landscapes carry stories across geological, ecological, and human timescales.

As visitors move through Maastricht, GPS automatically triggers audio stories connected to their surroundings.

Rather than following a fixed route, visitors discover stories by entering listening zones.
